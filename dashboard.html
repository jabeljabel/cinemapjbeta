⚠️ Key Distinction: Filmed vs. Represented Locations

FilmedLocationID

Where the camera actually was — physical shooting location (撮影地)

RepresentedLocationID

A place mentioned in the film's title, synopsis, subtitle, or plot

This distinction enables research on the "imagination gap" between where films are made and what places they depict.

Key relationships:

  • film_locations links films to locations (supports both FilmedLocationID and RepresentedLocationID)
  • directors and cast_members link people to films with role information
  • film_genres supports multiple genres per film with bilingual labels
  • synopses stores multiple synopsis versions per film in different languages
